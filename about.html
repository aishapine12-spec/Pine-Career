Our Story

Founded to close a gap no one else had addressed.

Pine Careers was founded on a conviction: the disconnect between student recruitment, alumni relationships, and industry engagement is not three small problems it is one systemic failure, repeated in every market. We built the integrated model to fix it, and we did the unglamorous work first: finding and verifying 15.4 million alumni across 28 countries, university by university, profile by profile.

In under a year, that foundation has produced partnerships with leading UK universities, five leadership roundtables across four Indian cities, and three consecutive years of delivered Turing Scheme cohorts the working proof of Re-engagement 2.0.

Our belief is simple: a university's presence in any market shouldn't end at enrollment. It should compound through the students it recruits, the alumni it retains, and the industry relationships it builds along the way.

Alongside Pine Careers sits Pine SETU our talent-facing platform, where students and professionals engage directly. The separation is deliberate: Pine Careers advises institutions with zero B2C conflict.
